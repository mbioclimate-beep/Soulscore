how to listen

five things to carry in

in order. never on shuffle. the order is the poem.

ask who is speaking. one voice fights, one answers.

ask who they speak to. first them. then, slowly, you.

listen to the silences. when the words stop, the story doesn’t.

don’t wait for peace at the end. wait for an alliance.

a promise

this is art.

a form I had to invent, because none of the old ones could hold this war.

I call it a soul score: songs that already exist, put in an order that never existed, so that two voices can find each other, and you can find yours.

what this does not claim

It reads one playlist; 32 of its first 39 songs carry enough words to count. A lexicon of 617 weighted words counts words and misses irony. Whom a song speaks to is a reading, not a measurement. Jung’s figures are names for the rooms, not a diagnosis. The epilogue (songs 40 to 47) came after the tests and is read by ear. The echoes are my associations, not sources.
